Company

As AI becomes a core competitive advantage, firms can no longer rely on off-the-shelf SaaS. Neither can they rely on fragile, quickly outdated software from builders who aren’t working at the frontier of AI.

Strange Loop is pioneering a new model.

We’re a team of applied AI engineers who partner with category-defining firms to build AI products that automate core operations end-to-end.

Each product captures the firm’s expertise, is owned entirely by the firm, and becomes a durable competitive advantage.

We operate and continuously improve these products as AI advances, backed by an enterprise-grade platform for monitoring, security, and compliance.

Team

We intend to only work with a select number of category-defining firms where we can deliver transformative value. As a result, we intend to keep our team lean, which lets us optimize for freedom, ownership, and upside:

  • We’re a team of generalist engineers, and only engineers. There are no salespeople, PMs, or managers, nor are there separate platform/ML/FDE teams. Engineers own the customer relationship, sit next to executives and experts, ship robust systems at extraordinary speed, and contribute back learnings from the field to our core platform.
  • We’re remote-first, offer complete freedom of tools, and keep process to a minimum.
  • A portion of profits are shared equally across the team, with no cap. In 2026, our profit share bonus was $500k.
