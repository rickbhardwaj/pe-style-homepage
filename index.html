Company

We believe a new category of software companies is emerging: the High Frequency Software Firm. These firms will resemble elite law firms more than traditional software vendors: lean teams with a reputation for exceptional talent and institutional trust will partner with top institutions to build products that absorb not only internal software budgets, but large portions of the operational labor.

We’re the first of these firms. We work with one firm per category (tax, private equity, law, etc.) and focus on making them win their category with proprietary, frontier AI products – and sharing the upside as we win together.

Strange Loop was founded in 2022 by Rick, Andrew, and Adam. We’re a team of Amazon AI engineers who helped launch Amazon Alexa and AWS’s first generation of AI services.

Careers

We’re building an organization that appeals to a rare kind of engineer: someone who can work alongside executives and domain experts and ship consequential systems at extraordinary speed. They are exceptional builders with unusual business instincts, often former or future founders like ourselves.

  • Maximum ownership: There are no salespeople, product managers, or layers of management. Engineers own the customer relationship and work directly with executives from the initial idea through deployment.
  • Maximum autonomy: We’re remote-first, offer complete freedom of tools, and keep process to a minimum. Given how selective we are with our engagements, we intend to keep our team small and talent-dense, which lets us optimize for freedom, speed, and autonomy.
  • Maximum upside: Profits are shared equally across the team, with no cap. In 2026, our profit share bonus was $100k. Our 2027 goal is $1M.
