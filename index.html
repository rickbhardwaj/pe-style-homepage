Team

Enterprise technology is broken, with each deployment fractured amongst platforms, sales teams, systems integrators, and internal IT. The result is predictable: roadmaps that age quickly, integrations that never end, and brittle products that fail to evolve with the people using them.

Strange Loop works differently. We’re a lean, talent-dense team of generalist engineers. We don’t have salespeople, PMs, or managers. Engineers own the customer relationship from beginning to end, working directly with executives and experts to discover problems, shipping robust agents at extraordinary speed, and carrying what they learn in the field back into our core platform.

Our org is designed around high trust and shared upside. We’re remote-first and keep process to a minimum. Profits are shared equally across the team, with average engineer comp on track to reach $1M+ by 2027.

In other words, we’re a team of future and former founders. If this sounds like the kind of company you want to build, we’d love to hear from you. Apply here.
